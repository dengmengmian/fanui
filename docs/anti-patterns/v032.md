# FanUI V0.3.2 Anti-pattern Additions

These anti-patterns extend `core.md` after Dogfood #3 rendered acceptance.

## 30. Fake Product Evidence

**Symptom:** a large product screenshot contains generic rows, empty panes, placeholder labels, or simplified boxes that do not resemble a real product state.

**Why it fails:** the evidence is large but not credible.

**Correction:** add context, navigation, selection, semantic state, realistic data, action hierarchy, and domain-specific detail.

## 31. Sparse Evidence in an Oversized Frame

**Symptom:** a large visual panel contains only a few tiny nodes or a narrow strip of information.

**Why it fails:** scale and information content are mismatched.

**Correction:** reduce the frame or increase the fidelity/density of the evidence until the visual area is earned.

## 32. Sidebar-edge Docs Alignment

**Symptom:** documentation content begins immediately after the sidebar while a very large unused field remains on the right.

**Why it fails:** the page is mathematically aligned but optically left-heavy.

**Correction:** center the content block inside the remaining reading canvas, accounting for TOC when present.

## 33. Editorial Placeholder Art

**Symptom:** Blog covers are generic pastel gradients with empty white rectangles or identical abstract tiles.

**Why it fails:** they read as skeletons and contribute no editorial meaning.

**Correction:** use domain-derived diagrams, traces, artifacts, code/data, or other idea-bearing visuals.

## 34. Un-earned Article Hero

**Symptom:** a large article Hero visual delays the first paragraph but contains little information.

**Why it fails:** visual area is consumed without improving comprehension or identity.

**Correction:** strengthen the visual, reduce it, or remove it.

## 35. Workspace Below-the-fold Void

**Symptom:** the core workspace occupies only the top part of the page, leaving a large blank area below.

**Why it fails:** a sustained working environment is treated like a web section.

**Correction:** make the workspace own remaining viewport height and distribute graph/runtime/detail panes through that space.

## 36. Semantic-color Collapse

**Symptom:** Running, Selected, Agent, Approval, and Primary Action all use the same brand color treatment.

**Why it fails:** color stops communicating operational meaning.

**Correction:** separate brand, selection, process, status, authority, and object-type roles.

## 37. Rounded Rectangle Everywhere

**Symptom:** Marketing frames, Blog covers, Pricing cards, Docs entries, Product panels, inputs, and tree rows all use roughly the same 12–16px rounded rectangle language.

**Why it fails:** surface hierarchy collapses and Product UI keeps a soft template feel.

**Correction:** apply radius by surface role; progressively reduce radius toward dense operational UI.

## 38. Tiny Product Typography

**Symptom:** dense UI achieves apparent sophistication by making navigation, metadata, rows, or inspector text difficult to read.

**Why it fails:** density becomes visual compression rather than productivity.

**Correction:** enforce the Minimum Readability Floor in `fidelity-density.md`.
