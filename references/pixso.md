# Reference: Pixso

## Role in FanUI

Pixso is the secondary reference for FanUI Web V0.1.

Its main role is to calibrate the system away from overly cold or purely utilitarian aesthetics and strengthen:

- brand expression;
- hero composition;
- product presentation;
- marketing section rhythm;
- visual richness;
- Chinese product storytelling.

## Sources reviewed

- https://pixso.cn/
- public product/feature pages and product presentation available from the website

Reviewed for FanUI Web V0.1 in September 2026.

## Observations

Pixso presents a broad product story spanning whiteboard, prototype, design, interaction demonstration, developer mode, AI, design system, community resources, solutions, enterprise scenarios, and collaboration.

The site uses more expressive visual composition than a dense professional Web App typically should.

Useful characteristics include:

- strong product-centered hero presentation;
- larger visual frames;
- generous marketing spacing;
- section-level background and composition changes;
- brand-forward use of imagery and color;
- explicit storytelling across product workflow stages;
- strong suitability for Chinese product copy and mixed design/technical vocabulary.

## FanUI extraction

Pixso supports several FanUI principles:

### Marketing may be expressive

FanUI marketing pages do not need to look like a desaturated admin dashboard. They may use stronger composition as long as hierarchy remains clear.

### Product story over feature dump

Capabilities should be grouped into user journeys or product stages where possible.

### Large evidence can replace extra copy

A strong product visual may communicate more than a long explanatory paragraph.

### Chinese content deserves native composition

Layouts should account for Chinese title width, dense characters, mixed Latin terminology, and enterprise copy without relying on English demo text.

## Relationship with Apifox

When Apifox and Pixso offer different useful qualities:

- prefer **Apifox** for professional-tool density, product calmness, complex navigation, and operational UI;
- prefer **Pixso** for marketing expression, visual storytelling, brand-forward sections, and product showcase composition.

FanUI should combine these strengths without visually cloning either product.
